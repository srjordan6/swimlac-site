Lakeside Fit is a new training group created for swimmers who love the sport but prefer a flexible pathway. This group is ideal for athletes who do not wish to commit to the full demands or who may not be able to meet the training requirements of the other Senior groups.

## USA Swimming Membership

All participants will be registered as USA Swimming members, giving them access to sanctioned opportunities within the North Texas LSC.

## Meet Participation

Lakeside Fit swimmers may participate in North Texas LSC meets, though meet opportunities will be limited to invited JSL or Block Party events. There is no meet participation requirement, allowing families to choose the level of involvement that best aligns with their schedules and goals.

## Coaching & Training Environment

The group will be led by a USA Swimming–certified coach, providing a fun, positive, and supportive environment. Athletes can enjoy swimming, develop skills, and stay active without the pressure of full-time competitive expectations.

## Focus & Philosophy

Lakeside Fit prioritizes fitness, technique development, and enjoyment of the sport. Swimmers will continue to grow in skill and confidence while maintaining the flexibility they need for school, other activities, or balanced schedules.

---

Lakeside Fit offers the best of both worlds—structured training with the Lakeside community, guided by certified coaching, and centered on fun, fitness, and flexibility.

## Tryout Requirement

All swimmers wishing to join LAC's competitive team must complete a [**tryout/placement evaluation**](http://www.teamunify.com/SubTabGeneric.jsp?team=ntlac&_stabid_=25927) to ensure proper group placement and training success.
