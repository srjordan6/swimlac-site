## Guest Swimmers

Any visiting USA Swimming (USAS) or FINA-affiliated swimmer is welcome to train with Lakeside Aquatic Club while on vacation or temporarily visiting the DFW area.

To request practice participation, please email our Team admins at teamadmin@swimlac.org with the following information:

- Swimmer’s first and last name, and age
- Proof of current USAS membership (e.g., Deck Pass screenshot) or FINA-affiliated membership
- Name of current swim team
- Preferred LAC training location (The Colony, Flower Mound, or Keller)
- Dates you will be in the area and wish to attend practice
- Proof of swim times so an appropriate training group may be assigned

Once all required information has been received, you will be notified of available practice days and times (pending space availability), as well as the coach to whom the swimmer is assigned.

Please note: Swimmers training with Lakeside Aquatic Club for more than two weeks will be subject to a monthly training fee.
