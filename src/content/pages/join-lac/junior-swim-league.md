## Mission Statement

The focus of the LAC Junior Swim League (JSL) is to enhance the racing opportunities and experience of members at the entry level of our competitive program. The JSL gives our Bronze and Silver athletes the opportunity to race in a meet format that is quick and fun.

## JSL Overview

The LAC Junior Swim League (JSL) is designed to give our athletes, who are entering the competitive program, a more frequent racing experience. The current structure for all members in North Texas Swimming LSC is to compete on a monthly basis. While competing once a month allows swimmers to train and make meaningful stroke improvements between meets, our coaching staff believes newer and novice swimmers benefit from more racing earlier in their career.

The LAC JSL allows our Bronze and Silver swimmers to compete 1-2 times a month during the short course season. Bronze and Silver swimmers are simultaneously learning how to race in meets and how to train in practice — both skills are essential for long-term development. Our coaching staff believes learning how to race needs to happen in a meet environment, which is why LAC modeled the Junior Swim League after high school dual meets.

Just like high school dual meets, JSL meets are not USA Swimming sanctioned meets. Swimmers' times will NOT be entered into the SWIMS database. Instead, swimmers focus on learning how to race in individual events. The JSL meets allow swimmers to compete 4-5 times prior to a USA Swimming sanctioned meet, and swimmers will participate in both types throughout the season.

Each JSL meet should last around 1.5 hours including warm-up, making them short and accessible for families new to swimming. Our USA Swimming sanctioned meets are typically held on a Saturday and Sunday and take 4-5 hours to complete. JSL meets are held at locations close to each athlete's home pool to minimize travel for families.

Our coaching staff believes these JSL meets will be a fun and positive environment to help foster the love of swimming and racing in our younger athletes, and that added race opportunities will allow swimmers to improve faster.

## JSL Information

The LAC JSL consists of swimmers from the Bronze and Silver groups. Swimmers in the bronze groups will compete primarily in JSL meets rather than monthly USA Swimming sanctioned meets. They will participate in specific USA Swimming sanctioned meets during the season, but the focus during the short course season will be on JSL meets.

Swimmers in the silver groups have the option to compete in both JSL and monthly USA Swimming sanctioned meets. Our coaching staff encourages Silver swimmers to compete in at least one JSL meet per month and a USA Swimming meet every other month. The coaches of each Silver group will review which meets each swimmer should attend at the beginning of the season so families can plan their schedules accordingly.

## Meet Format

Swimmers compete individually in a 6 or 8 lane pool at one of our sites: Northwest, Keller, Flower Mound, The Colony and Southlake. Swimmers are timed using stopwatches rather than an electronic timing system.

Each meet begins with a 20-minute warm-up. Events vary from meet to meet but typically include a combination of the following: 50 Freestyle, 50 Backstroke, 50 Butterfly, 50 Breaststroke, and either the 100 Freestyle or 100 IM. Relay events may also be included.

Each swimmer will compete in either 1 relay and 3 individual events, or 2 relays and 2 individual events. Relay assignments are made by the coaching staff.

## Awards

Heat winner awards will be given to individual swimmers. Award winners will be invited to the podium area for a photo that will be posted on LAC social media.

## Cost

The cost is a $10 surcharge and $10 per event.
