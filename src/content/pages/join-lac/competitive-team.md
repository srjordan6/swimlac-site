Members of the Lakeside Aquatic Club competitive team train within either the **Age Group Program** or the **Senior Program**, as outlined below.

## Age Group Program (12 & Under)

The Age Group Program is designed for swimmers ages **12 and under**. Athletes may enter this program through a **tryout/placement evaluation** or by **invitation from the LAC Stroke School**.

The program includes four progressive levels:  
**Bronze → Silver → Gold → Age Group Select (AGS)**

As swimmers advance through these levels, they take on **increasingly challenging skills, training expectations, and commitment requirements**.

## Senior Program (13 & Older)

The Senior Program is intended for swimmers ages **13 and older**.  
Athletes are placed into groups based on **maturity**, **training ability**, **attendance**, and **meet performance**. The Senior Program consists of multiple training groups, including:

- **Senior Prep**
- **Senior**
- **National Prep**
- **National**

Each group provides training appropriate to the athlete's developmental stage and competitive goals.

## Competitive Team Participation

Swimmers in the competitive program are eligible to attend **USA Swimming–sanctioned meets**, as well as LAC camps, clinics, select teams, and all membership events.

Being part of LAC's competitive team means that **both swimmers and their families** agree to follow all **membership policies** and fulfill the **volunteer requirements** that support team operations and hosted events.

## Tryout Requirement

All swimmers wishing to join LAC's competitive team must complete a [**tryout/placement evaluation**](http://www.teamunify.com/SubTabGeneric.jsp?team=ntlac&_stabid_=25927) to ensure proper group placement and training success.
