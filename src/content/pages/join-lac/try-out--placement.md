Thank you for your interest in Lakeside Aquatic Club! We look forward to meeting your swimmer.

LAC is a year-round competitive swim team. All swimmers must be evaluated by our coaching staff to determine the appropriate training group placement. We offer 15 group levels based on age and ability.

---

## Tryout Requirements

To participate in tryouts, swimmers must be able to:

- Swim **50 yards freestyle**
- Swim **25 yards** of backstroke, butterfly, and breaststroke

If your swimmer cannot yet complete these distances, please register for our **Lesson Program**, which prepares swimmers for our Pre-Competitive Stroke Team.

## What to Bring

Please print and complete [**THIS FORM**](http://www.teamunify.com/ntlac/UserFiles/Image/QuickUpload/lac-swimmer-evaluation-and-placement_021431.pdf) and bring it to tryouts.  
Swimmers should arrive **ready to swim** (suit, goggles, cap if needed).

Parents may observe from the lobby or bleachers but may not be on deck.

---

## Important Tryout Policies

- **Try out at your assigned location.**  
  Swimmers must attend the tryout for the location where they would train. New swimmers cannot request a location transfer for one year.
- **Arrive on time.**  
  Late arrivals cannot be accommodated.
- **Low-stress evaluation.**  
  Coaches will simply observe skills and basic drills. This is not a race or competitive event.

---

## Notification Timeline

You will receive your swimmer's status by email within **two weeks**. We do not provide individual feedback.

Possible results:

1. **Invitation to join** and group assignment
2. **Waitlist** placement
3. **No available group**, with recommendations for other programs

---

## Waitlist Information

Space is limited at each level. Waitlists are used only when we expect to onboard swimmers within the current season; we do not keep swimmers on waitlists indefinitely.

Waitlist movement depends on:

- Lane space and facility schedules
- Group attendance
- Swimmers moving between levels

Because of these factors, we cannot estimate your swimmer's position or timeline. We also **do not offer private lessons** for swimmers waiting for tryouts or in waitlist status.

## Transfer Swimmers

If your swimmer is currently registered with USA Swimming and has a solid competition history, email **teamadmin@swimlac.org** with:

- Swimmer's name
- Age
- USAS ID# and expiration
- Current team
- LAC location of interest
