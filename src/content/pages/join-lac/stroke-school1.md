## About Stroke School

Stroke School is part of the **Development** segment within LAC's three-tier swimming pathway:  
**Learn-To-Swim → Stroke School → Competitive Team**

The purpose of Stroke School is to strengthen the fundamental techniques of all four competitive strokes and prepare swimmers for entry into LAC's Competitive Team. Stroke School consists of two training groups:

- **Red Group** (entry level)
- **Black Group** (advanced pre-competitive)

Age ranges may vary by location or season based on available space.

---

## RED GROUP

### **Red 1** — 8 & Under

### **Red 2** — Ages 9–11*

The Red Group is the **first level** of Stroke School. Many swimmers advancing to Red come from LAC's Learn-To-Swim program, while others are placed here through tryouts.

Swimmers in this group should be able to:

- Swim freestyle with proper side breathing
- Swim backstroke with good body rotation
- Demonstrate a basic understanding of breaststroke and butterfly
- Begin learning block starts and proper freestyle flip turns

### Progression Requirements: Red → Black

To move into the Black Group, swimmers must be able to:

- Perform all freestyle and backstroke drills correctly
- Complete a legal freestyle flip turn
- Swim a **legal 25 breaststroke** *or* **25 butterfly**

***Age limit may vary by location**

---

## BLACK GROUP

### **Black 1** — 9 & Under

### **Black 2** — Ages 10–12*

Swimmers in the Black Group focus on:

- Perfecting breaststroke and butterfly technique
- Learning and performing a legal **backstroke flip turn**
- Increasing endurance through longer freestyle and backstroke swims
- Learning to read the **pace clock**
- Fine-tuning stroke mechanics across all four competitive strokes

To be considered for placement into the Competitive Team (Bronze Group), swimmers must be able to:

- Legally swim all four competitive strokes
- Perform a legal **backstroke flip turn**
- Complete the following test sets:

### Black Group Test Sets

- **6 × 50 freestyle on 1:30**
- **100 IM legally** (all strokes, turns, and finishes must be legal)

***Age limit may vary by location**

## Stroke School Meets

Red and Black swimmers participate in **fun speed & skill meets**, typically held 5-6 times per season. These low-stress events help swimmers and parents learn the meet format while allowing swimmers to apply their technique in a positive, beginner-friendly environment.
