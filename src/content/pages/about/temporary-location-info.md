## Southlake / Carroll ISD Aquatic Center (CISD)

**Address:**
1501 W. Southlake Blvd
Southlake, TX 76092

The Aquatic Center is located just east of Carroll Senior High School.

### Parking & Drop-Off

- Parking is available behind the building.
- For swimmer pick-up and drop-off, please use the pick-up line road on the south end of the building near the front entrance.
- Please do not stop on the west-side entrance road, as this often causes traffic congestion.

### Parent Viewing & Seating

- CISD, like Keller, does not allow parents on deck.
- Parents may watch practices from the upstairs bleachers.
- Swimmers may store their bags and sit in the bleacher area underneath the upstairs seating.

## Hidden Lakes / Bear Creek Pool

**Address:**
2700 Bear Creek Parkway
Keller, TX

The pool is located next to the Hidden Lakes community clubhouse.

### Facility Access

- The pool area is secured with fencing and electronic gate locks.
- Please do not climb the fence.
- Do not enter the pool area until a coach is on deck.

## Highland Park High School Natatorium

**Location:**
Directly across the street from Highland Park High School
Dallas, TX 75205

### Parking

- Parking spots outside the building are available after 5:00 PM.
- Prior to 5:00 PM, street parking is available along Douglass and Grasmere.

## Texas Woman’s University (TWU)

**1600 N Bell Avenue, Denton, TX, 76209 -** **Pioneer Hall**

### Parking & Drop-Off

- Swimmer drop-off should take place on the north side of Pioneer Hall using the driveway designated for deliveries and maintenance.
- Swimmers should enter through the north door, walk down the hallway, and access the pool area.
- After drop-off, parents should park in the large lot just north of the building. There is a stoplight at the entrance to the lot.
- Although the lot is marked as "permit parking," parking is not enforced after 5:00 PM.
- Please do not park in reserved or handicap spaces unless properly permitted.
- Please do not stop on Bell Avenue for drop-off, and do not park in the driveway area. That area is for loading and unloading only.

### Parent Viewing & Facility Access

- Parents are welcome inside the building.
- There is upstairs seating with a full view of the pool through the glass walls.
- Pool deck space is limited, so only swimmers and staff should remain on deck unless necessary.

### Pool Entry

- Everyone should enter through the main interior doors on the north side of the pool.
- Please do not use the exterior south-side pool doors.
