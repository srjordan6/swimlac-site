## Membership Fees

Lakeside Aquatic Club (LAC) is a year-round program. All swimmers must register with the club annually. The initial LAC Registration Fee is charged when you join and again each late summer during our annual membership renewal. During this renewal, families update their information, pay yearly fees, and complete USA Swimming registration for the upcoming year.

---

## LAC Annual Registration Fee

(One fee per household)

Assessed upon joining and again each summer for returning members.

- **Competitive Team:** $200
- **Pre-Competitive Team:** $100

---

## USA Swimming Registration Fee

Required for all competitive swimmers (Bronze groups and above).

- **Annual Fee:** $100, collected during fall registration renewal
- **Transfers:** Swimmers transferring from another USA Swimming club may owe a **$6 transfer fee** (conditions apply)

---

## Monthly Membership Dues

- Assessed on the **1st of each month**
- A **$10 late fee** is applied if payment is received after the **15th**
- Families with multiple swimmers receive a **10% discount** for each additional swimmer
  - The discount applies to the lower-priced group(s)
  - Discount applies to **monthly dues only**

Monthly fees vary by training group.

---

## Meet Entry Fee Deposit

All swimmers in **Bronze and above** are billed a **$75 deposit** to cover meet entry fees if payment is not received before the meet.

- Required regardless of meet participation
- Actual meet entry charges will be billed to your account
- The $75 deposit is **refunded when the swimmer leaves LAC**

Refunds

- Once a meet entry deadline has passed, **meet entry fees are non-refundable**
- Membership dues reflect participation on the team, not specific practice availability
- No refunds are provided for canceled or modified practices due to factors outside our control, including:
  - Facility closures
  - Weather
  - Mechanical issues
  - Outside events at rented facilities

## Withdrawing from LAC

To withdraw, parents must notify the **Team Manager in writing** at **[teamadmin@swimlac.org](mailto:teamadmin@swimlac.org)** .
A **30-day notice** is required to avoid additional billing.
Notifying the coaching staff does **not** meet this requirement.
