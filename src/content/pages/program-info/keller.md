**Temporary Locations Info:**

**<u>Southlake aka Carroll ISD Aquatic Center, aka CISD,</u>**

1501 W. Southlake Blvd, 76092

The Aquatic center is just east of the Carroll Senior High.  Parking is available behind the building.   For pick up and drop off use the pick up line road on the south end of the building where the front doors are, <u>please do not stop on the entrance road on the west side</u> of the building that usually causes the most problems.

CISD, like Keller, doesn't allow parents on deck. The upstairs bleachers are available to parents for viewing.  Swimmers can sit and put their bags on the bleachers under the upstairs seating.

**<u>NW aka Northwest ISD Aquatic Center aka NWISD aka Justin</u>**

1976 Texan Dr, Northlake, TX 76247

The Aquatic Center is just South of the football field.  Parking is available in front of the building.  Pick up and drop off is at the main entrance of the facility.

NWISD, like Keller, doesn't allow parents on deck. The upstairs bleachers are available to parents for viewing.  Swimmers can sit and put their bags on the bleachers around the pools.

**<u>Hidden Lakes aka Bear Creek Pool- Hidden Lakes neighborhood of Keller</u>**

2700 Bear Creek Parkway, Keller Texas

The pool is next to the community clubhouse.  The pool area is secured by fencing and electronic gate locks.  Please don’t climb the fence. **<mark>Do enter the pool area until a coach is on deck.</mark>**
