**Additional TKP FAQs**

**Can my swimmers play before and after the lesson? (TKP)** Members of the Keller Pointe may use the pool before and after lessons. However, swimmers need to come over to the LAC area 5 minutes before class starts. After class, they need to check in with their parent before continuing to swim. If you are not a member and you would like to use the pool outside of the lesson time, visit the front desk for a day pass.

**Where should parents wait? (TKP)** This is the only facility that allows parents on deck. However, we would like to maintain a certain level of professionalism even though we are at a REC pool. We ask that the chairs next to the lesson area are left open for the swimmers to sit before and after lessons. Parents can continue past that area and find a seat further down the deck. It is a distraction for the swimmers and the coaches to have the parents right behind the lesson area. We want everyone to get the most out of their lesson time. The swimmers need to focus on their teacher. And the teacher needs to focus on their swimmers.
