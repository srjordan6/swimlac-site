## **About Us**

Thank you for your interest in Lakeside Aquatic Club (LAC)! Our swim school operates at four locations across North Texas and offers a well-rounded, year-round lesson program. Under the leadership of **Sarah Crouch**, our Swim School Director, LAC has developed a recognized teaching progression that emphasizes water safety and provides the skills needed to transition into competitive swimming. Since 2012, this program has helped thousands of swimmers build confidence and strong technical foundations.

---

## **LAC Lesson Program**

Our Developmental Program is designed to give every swimmer the fundamentals of proper stroke technique and efficient movement in the water. Whether your goal is water safety, improving swimming confidence, or beginning the path toward competitive swimming, our curriculum supports swimmers at all levels.

### **Program Features**

- **Year-round lessons** at multiple sites
- Swimmers are **evaluated on the first day** to determine proper placement
- **10-level progression** that builds skills for all four competitive strokes
- **Continuous advancement:** swimmers move to the next level as soon as they master skills—no waiting for a new session
- **Small class sizes** (maximum 5 swimmers per instructor)

## **Path to Stroke Team**

Once swimmers complete all 10 lesson levels, they may be invited to join the **Stroke Team**, LAC’s pre-competitive program.

- Swimmers are placed based on **age and skill**
- The first level is **Red Team**, followed by **Black Team**
- Schedules can be found at **[www.swimlac.org](http://www.swimlac.org)**
- Age ranges vary by location

---

## **Path to Competitive Club Team**

Swimmers who complete all Black 1/Black 2 skills and pass the required test sets may be invited to join LAC’s club program.

- Placement is based on **age, readiness, and skill mastery**
- For swimmers beginning lessons at age 11–12, the path may take longer
- Families with older swimmers may contact their site’s **Deck Manager** for guidance
