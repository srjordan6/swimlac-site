## Lakeside Aquatic Club Overview

Lakeside Aquatic Club (LAC) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit swim organization serving Keller, Flower Mound, Justin, and The Colony, Texas. As one of the leading swim programs in the state, LAC offers a full range of training opportunities, including swim lessons, stroke development, and competitive team programs.

With a structure that supports swimmers from beginner through national-level competition, LAC provides an environment where athletes of all ages and abilities can develop skills, build confidence, and achieve their goals in the sport.

## History & Achievements

Founded in 1982, Lakeside Aquatic Club began as a summer recreational swim team before rapidly evolving into a highly competitive program.

Early investment in experienced coaching helped accelerate the club's growth, leading to participation in National and Junior National competitions within just a few years.

Today, LAC serves more than 1,300 swimmers and is recognized as one of the largest and most successful swim clubs in Texas.

The club has received multiple honors from USA Swimming, including:

- Gold Medal Club status, awarded to top-performing clubs in the United States

## Programs & Training Pathways

Lakeside Aquatic Club offers a comprehensive progression designed to support swimmers at every stage of development.

### Learn-to-Swim

This program introduces fundamental water safety skills and basic swimming techniques. It is designed for beginners who are building confidence and comfort in the water.

### Stroke School / Development

This level focuses on refining technique across all four competitive strokes while improving endurance and efficiency. It prepares swimmers for transition into team training.

### Competitive Team

The competitive program supports athletes pursuing higher levels of performance. Swimmers train and compete at local, regional, and national levels, with structured groups for both age-group and senior athletes.

## Coaching & Staff

All coaching staff are certified through USA Swimming, ensuring a high standard of safety, professionalism, and athlete development.

The coaching team's experience and leadership have contributed to the development of swimmers who have advanced to state, sectional, national, and international levels of competition.
