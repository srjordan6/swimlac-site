## USA Swimming Motivational Time Standards

[2025–2028 USA Swimming National Age Group Motivational Time Standards (B, BB, A, etc.)](https://www.gomotionapp.com/ntlac/UserFiles/Image/QuickUpload/2028-motivational-standards-age-group_066754.pdf)

These are the charts used to determine what time-standard level your swimmer has achieved and which meets they're eligible for. The PDF is organized by course type:

- **Pages 1–2:** Short course yards (used mainly in the winter)
- **Pages 3–4:** Short course meters (we rarely race in a 25-meter pool, so you can ignore these)
- **Pages 5–6:** Long course meters (what we use mostly in the summer)

For NT, these time standards went into effect on April 1, 2025.
