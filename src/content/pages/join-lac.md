Lakeside Aquatic Club (LAC) offers a comprehensive, year-round swimming pathway made up of three programs: **Learn-To-Swim**, **Stroke School**, and the **Competitive Team**. Each program builds upon the skills developed in the previous level. Our mission is to create water-safe athletes and provide the technique, training, and support needed to excel at every stage of swimming.

Use the information below to learn more about each program.

---

## Learn-To-Swim (Lessons Program)

The Learn-To-Swim program is a swimmer's first introduction to structured lessons. Swimmers learn foundational water skills including floating, breathing, kicking, arm movement, and water independence.

This program serves swimmers of **all ages** and is offered at select LAC locations.

### Key Information

- Designed for swimmers **not yet proficient in all four strokes for 25 yards**
- No tryout is required
- Swimmers are **evaluated on the first day** and placed in appropriate skill groups
- Registration is completed **online**

## Stroke School (Pre-Competitive Program)

Stroke School is the introductory step toward being on a competitive swim team. It is generally designed for swimmers **12 and younger** (may vary by location or season based on space).

In Stroke School, swimmers learn:

- Circle swimming
- How to read a pace clock
- Basic competition rules
- Breath timing
- Body rotation
- Stroke drills
- Participation in fun, intrasquad-style beginner meets

### Tryout Requirement

A tryout is required for placement into Stroke School.  
If swimmers cannot swim **25 yards of all four competitive strokes**, they should begin in Learn-To-Swim instead.

LAC typically holds open tryouts in **late summer** and **early winter**.

## Competitive Team

LAC's Competitive Team prepares swimmers to compete at the **local, regional, national, and international** levels. The program is divided into:

- **Age Group Program (4 levels):** typically for swimmers **13 and under**
- **Senior Program (4 levels):** typically for swimmers **13 and older**

### Placement Requirements

All swimmers must complete a **Try-Out/Placement Evaluation** to determine the appropriate training group.

### Volunteer Requirement

Families of Competitive Team swimmers must complete a **yearly volunteer obligation** at LAC-hosted meets.
